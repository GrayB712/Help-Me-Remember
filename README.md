> **She's been seeing three mysterious figures looming over her in her dreams. They seem almost familiar, but she doesn't recognize them. You need to help her remember anything she can about them.**
> <br></br>

### Welcome to the repo for Help Me Remember! This is where the latest (frequently unstable) version of the game can be found.

**About this Game:** Help Me Remember was built in Unity/C# in 4 days for the 2026 GMTK Game Jam. Miraculously, it scored in the top 3% for the narrative category!

**Post-Jam Release:** A post-jam version of Help Me Remember is currently in production. The projected completion date is ~December 2025. 

**Credits:** Big thanks to my mom for helping me pick out which song titles should be on the radio. (She also helped write a lot of the diary entries).
